Privacy Policy

Sabolog (the “App”) respects your privacy and strives to provide a service you can use with confidence. This policy explains how the App handles data.

1. Basic policy

The App does not send or collect your entered slacking tasks, categories, or other in-app records on servers operated by the developer. This data is generally stored on your device.

If you use iCloud backups, the backed-up data is stored in your own iCloud storage associated with your Apple Account. The App may also use advertising services such as Google AdMob. Advertising providers may collect advertising identifiers, device information, usage information, and other data related to serving ads.

2. Information handled by the App

The App may save information you enter on your device to provide its features. This information is used to provide, display, record, and restore app data. The developer does not send, store, or view your in-app data on external servers.

3. Where data is stored

App data is generally stored on your device. If you enable iCloud backups, backed-up data is stored in your own iCloud storage. Data management, synchronization, storage, and restoration in iCloud use Apple's iCloud services. The developer cannot view, obtain, or manage backups stored in your iCloud.

4. iCloud backups

The App may provide iCloud backup features for your convenience. Backups are stored in your own iCloud storage associated with your Apple Account. The developer cannot view, obtain, or manage your iCloud backup data.

5. Notifications

The App may provide notifications based on your settings. Notifications are managed on your device. You can change notification permission, disable notifications, or adjust their time in your device settings or in the App.

6. Advertising

The App may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. Advertising providers may collect the following information to display ads, measure their effectiveness, prevent misuse, or optimize advertising:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP addresses
  • App usage information
  • Information about ad impressions and interactions
  • Crash logs and performance information

Each advertising provider's privacy policy governs its handling of this information. Your entered task names and other in-app records are not sent to advertising providers through the developer's servers.

For details about Google's handling of data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob.

7. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not provide your task information or other in-app records to third parties, except where required by law. However, third-party advertising providers may collect information necessary to serve ads.

8. Data transmitted outside your device

The App does not transmit your entered tasks or other in-app records to servers operated by the developer. Data may be stored or transmitted outside your device in these situations:

  • You use iCloud backups.
  • Third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob collect information necessary to serve ads.
  • The operating system or advertising SDKs collect crash logs, diagnostics, or performance information.

9. Deleting data

You can delete registered tasks, completed tasks, and other data using the App's features. Deleting the App from your device also deletes the App's locally stored data. If you use iCloud backups, you may need to delete data stored in iCloud through your device or iCloud settings.

10. Security

The App strives to handle your data appropriately. Data is generally stored on your device or, if you use iCloud backups, in your own iCloud storage. Due to the nature of internet communications, devices, cloud services, and advertising services, complete security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact leo.indieappworks+sabolog@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

The developer may change this policy in response to changes in law, service content, features, or advertising services. Changes will be announced appropriately in the App or on a public webpage.

Effective date: June 3, 2026
