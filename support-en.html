Sabolog Support

Sabolog is a home for the tasks you choose to put off.

Contact

For questions, feedback, or support, email leo.indieappworks+sabolog@gmail.com. Please include your app version, iOS version, and a description of the issue. You do not need to send private task records.

Purchases

Sabolog Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription and includes no ads, automatic backups, app icon customization, and Original/Simple styles. The separate one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads only.

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro page with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings on your iPhone: your name > Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backups

Manual iCloud backups are available without Pro. Automatic backups require Pro. The app retains up to five backup versions in total. iCloud backups require iCloud to be available for your Apple Account.
